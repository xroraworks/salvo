About Salvo Nicolosi

Photographs that keep their meaning.

I'm Salvo Nicolosi, a photographer based in Catania, working across weddings, events and commercial projects.

My approach combines reportage and direction, with a focus on natural, purposeful images.

01 depth of field

Experience shaped by people and purpose.

Field experience and training at Studio Fotografico Sicurella shaped my approach to photography.

A Degree and a Master's in Digital Marketing added a broader perspective on communication and purpose.

Working frame

Natural photographs require intention.

I listen, read the context, then decide when to step back and when to guide. The goal is to preserve atmosphere, identity and trust.

02 method

A calm presence and clear direction.

Every assignment starts with understanding the people, the context and how the photographs will be used.

From there, I keep the process simple: preparation when needed, observation when possible and direction when useful.

03 territory

From Catania, across Sicily and beyond.

I work mainly in Catania and across Sicily, with availability for weddings, events and projects throughout Italy and abroad.

Let's talk about what you have in mind.

Tell me about your event or project and we'll start from there.
