Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit salvonicolosiph.com, contact Salvo Nicolosi Photography or interact with analytics and advertising tools used on the website.

Last updated: 2 September 2026

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller is Salvo Nicolosi, photographer and owner of Salvo Nicolosi Photography, based in Catania, Sicily, Italy.

For any request concerning privacy or the processing of personal data, you can contact: info@salvonicolosiph.com .

2. Personal data processed

Depending on how the website is used, the following categories of personal data may be processed:

  • technical and browsing data, such as IP address, device information, browser type and interaction data;
  • data voluntarily provided when contacting Salvo Nicolosi Photography by email or WhatsApp;
  • information relating to interactions with the website, including page views, clicks and navigation events;
  • identifiers and information collected through analytics and advertising technologies, where the user has provided the required consent.

3. Contact requests

When you contact Salvo Nicolosi Photography by email or WhatsApp, the information you voluntarily provide is processed in order to respond to your request, provide information about services and, where requested, prepare quotations or manage a professional relationship.

The legal basis for this processing is the performance of pre-contractual measures requested by the data subject and, where applicable, the performance of a contract.

4. Analytics and website measurement

The website may use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with its pages and to measure website performance.

Depending on the configuration adopted, analytics technologies may process information about visits, pages viewed, interactions, devices and approximate geographic information.

Where required by applicable law, these technologies are activated only after the user has provided consent through the website's consent management system.

5. Advertising, remarketing and profiling

With the user's prior consent, the website may use advertising technologies such as the Meta Pixel and other advertising tools to measure campaign performance, understand interactions with advertisements and create audiences for remarketing or personalised advertising.

These technologies may process identifiers, browsing information, interactions with the website and information relating to the device or browser used.

Advertising and profiling technologies are not activated before the user has expressed the required consent.

6. Conversion and interaction measurement

The website may measure certain interactions, such as clicks on contact buttons, email links, WhatsApp links or other relevant actions, in order to understand the effectiveness of the website and advertising campaigns.

Where these measurements involve non-essential tracking technologies, they are performed only after the appropriate consent has been provided.

7. Links to social networks and external services

The website contains links to external platforms including Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

When a user follows one of these links, they leave this website and become subject to the privacy practices and terms of the relevant third-party service.

8. Recipients and service providers

Personal data may be processed by service providers used to operate, secure, analyse and promote the website, including hosting, analytics, advertising and communication providers.

These providers process personal data according to their role as independent controllers or processors and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

9. International data transfers

Some service providers used by the website may process personal data outside the European Economic Area.

Where such transfers occur, appropriate safeguards required by applicable data protection law are used, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, where applicable.

10. Data retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

Specific retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, the service used and the existence of contractual, administrative or legal requirements.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, where applicable, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability.

You may also object to certain processing activities and withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Requests may be sent to info@salvonicolosiph.com .

12. Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes applicable data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

13. Cookies and tracking technologies

Further information about cookies, analytics technologies, advertising tools and consent preferences is available in the Cookie Policy .

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, the services used or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page together with its last update date.
