The celebration stays at the centre

Good birthday photography records the event without making guests feel that they are constantly being directed.

The coverage balances group photographs and key moments with candid images that show the character of the party.

What the service includes

Every assignment is adapted to the event, the intended use and the people involved.

Who it is for
Adults, families and organisers planning a birthday or milestone celebration.
What is covered
Guests, portraits, decorations, entertainment, cake, speeches, dancing and spontaneous interactions.
How it works
Key moments are agreed before the event, with flexible coverage once the celebration begins.

A complete edited gallery ready for sharing, personal archives and selected prints.
